# Code of Conduct

kaleidoscope welcomes everyone. Contributors and maintainers agree to:

* be respectful, patient and constructive;
* welcome people of every background, identity and experience level;
* give and accept feedback about code, not about people;
* not harass, insult, threaten or discriminate against anyone;
* not publish anyone's private information without their permission.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainer through the contact
details on the project website. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove comments, code or contributors that break this code of
conduct.
